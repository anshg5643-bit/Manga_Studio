How a page gets read.

Find the text

An open-source OCR model returns every line of text with its box.

Group balloons

Boxes are painted onto the white areas of the page. Lines sharing one enclosed shape become one balloon.

Keep story text

A small trained classifier drops sound effects, page numbers and signs.

Order and name

Balloons are cut into tiers, read right to left, and speakers keep one name across pages.
